# Search terms for Parallel Monitor and deep research

Compiled 18 November 2026 from the live news blog, the Book of News and four Parallel runs. Update this list on each run when new product names appear.

## 1. Ready-to-paste Monitor query

Parallel Monitor watches the web on a schedule and alerts on new matches. The connected Parallel tools here have no Monitor function, so paste this into Parallel Monitor yourself.

Query:

```
New or updated Microsoft product and service announcements from Microsoft Ignite 2026 (17-20 November 2026), published on news.microsoft.com (Ignite live news and Book of News), blogs.microsoft.com, azure.microsoft.com/blog, techcommunity.microsoft.com, microsoft.ai/news, blogs.windows.com, github.blog/changelog or learn.microsoft.com. Report each announcement with product, what changed, release status (GA, public preview, private preview, Frontier, coming soon, retirement, pricing), availability date or regions, and the source URL. Include status changes to items already announced at Ignite 2026, such as preview moving to GA. Ignore third-party commentary and pre-November 2026 news.
```

Suggested cadence: every 8 hours during Ignite week (to match the 3 runs a day), then daily until 31 December 2026 to catch December GA dates.

Second Monitor (licensing and partner changes):

```
Microsoft licensing, pricing, Copilot Credits, Microsoft 365 E5/E7, Copilot Business, CSP and Microsoft AI Cloud Partner Program changes announced from 17 November 2026, from learn.microsoft.com/partner-center/announcements, microsoft.com/licensing/news or official Microsoft blogs. Report the change, effective date, who it affects and the source URL.
```

## 2. Ready-to-paste deep research prompt (run once, high effort)

```
Build a complete, de-duplicated catalogue of product and service announcements made at Microsoft Ignite 2026 (17-20 November 2026). Use the Book of News (https://news.microsoft.com/events/ignite/2026/book-of-news/) and the live news blog (https://news.microsoft.com/events/ignite/2026/live-news/) as primary sources, then official Microsoft blogs. For each announcement give: product family, product, capability, short description, status (GA, public preview, private preview, Frontier, coming soon, retirement, pricing), availability date and regions, audience, licensing prerequisites, and official source URL. Pay extra attention to Power Platform, Teams, SharePoint, Purview, Intune, Edge for Business, Security Copilot, licensing and industry solutions. Flag anything not verified on an official Microsoft page.
```
