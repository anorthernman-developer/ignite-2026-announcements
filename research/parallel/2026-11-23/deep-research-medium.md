**Scope and completeness.** Microsoft describes the Book of News as a guide to key announcements; the live news blog records keynote announcements. Together, the sources support the catalogue below, but they do not provide every requested field for every capability. Some entries bundle many features, and several official posts give no regional rollout, licensing, or exact date. I have marked those fields “not stated” rather than infer them. Accordingly, this is a de-duplicated catalogue of the announcements I could verify in the official material—not a claim that every one of the Book’s reported 59 entries and every sub-feature is itemized here.

**How to read the entries:** each row gives family/product; capability and short description; status; availability/date and regions; audience; licensing prerequisites; and the official Microsoft source. “Not stated” means the cited official material does not specify it. Preview, Frontier, pilot, and GA are kept distinct.

### Licensing, pricing, retirement, and industry-solution checks

- **Microsoft IQ:** Microsoft says it is built on products customers already use and requires no new SKU or subscription.
- **Agent 365 Business Analytics:** the Book states the tenant-level Copilot-license requirement for Microsoft services and Agent 365 requirement for non-Microsoft services; it does not state a new price for this Ignite announcement.
- **Power Platform licensing:** the Ignite Power Platform post does not set out licensing prerequisites for the items summarized above; Power BI custom data apps are specifically described as preview access for Power BI Pro customers.
- **Retirements and new product pricing:** I could not verify a specific Ignite-announced retirement or new price from the official Book/live excerpts cited here. I have therefore not imported unrelated retirement notices or prices into this event catalogue.
- **Industry solutions:** the official coverage includes business-use examples, but the reviewed Ignite sources do not establish a distinct healthcare, manufacturing, retail, or other industry product launch with its own release status and licensing terms. Treat any such claim as **not verified as an Ignite product announcement**.
- **Security Copilot:** I could not verify a distinct new Security Copilot product announcement with Ignite-specific status, availability, regions, and license terms in the official material cited here. Existing Security Copilot documentation is not evidence of a new Ignite launch.

**Items needing official-page confirmation before procurement or rollout:** features with no published region, precise date, or licensing prerequisite above; the exact availability of the ERP-specific Work IQ scope because Microsoft’s official posts differ in wording; and any claimed Security Copilot, industry-specific, price, or retirement announcement not directly confirmed in an official Microsoft Ignite/product page. The primary references are the [Book of News](https://news.microsoft.com/events/ignite/2026/book-of-news/) and [live news blog](https://news.microsoft.com/events/ignite/2026/live-news/).
